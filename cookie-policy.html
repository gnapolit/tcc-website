Welcome to www.thecostieracollective.com. This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website, including our blog on agency activities for creators, influencers, and professionals in the industry.

2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files saved on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They help improve your browsing experience, analyze site performance, and deliver more relevant content.

3. Cookies we use

First-party cookies — Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics cookies to analyze visits, understand how users interact with our content, and improve site performance. These cookies do not collect personally identifiable information and are processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookie Purpose Type
_ga Identifies unique users First-party
_gid Manages rate limiting First-party

Third-party cookie — Instagram

We use 1 Instagram cookie when embedded content (e.g. posts, feed) from our Instagram profile is displayed on the site. This cookie is managed by Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited) and is subject to Instagram's Privacy Policy.

4. How we manage cookies

Analytics and preference cookies: Google Analytics and Instagram cookies require your consent before activation. You can manage your preferences via the consent banner that appears on your first visit, or by adjusting your browser settings at any time.
